Packaging Drop-Test Control

Damage Prevention → Claims Control → Brand Protection
Reality

Most failures are not “product defects”

USA distribution punishes weak packaging. Ratings, returns, and chargebacks are usually caused by handling cycles, not factory defects. Packaging is a procurement risk layer — not an afterthought.

  • Parcel: drops, edge impacts, conveyor friction
  • Pallet: fork damage, stacking compression, container vibration
  • Returns: re-pack cycles that destroy margins
Defense Logic

ISTA-style survivability architecture

We design packaging to survive the path you actually run: marketplace parcel, LTL, retail DC, cross-dock, and returns.

  • Carton architecture: corner strength + compression logic
  • Internal structure: foam density + void control + part isolation
  • Unboxing: brand narrative + damage reduction alignment
